Optional add-ons: Reforged*.big archives the game mounts next to generals.exe.
Put ReforgedTextures.big here (Reforged's HD textures, from the official launcher's "HD Textures" add-on).
The launcher copies it to every PC; each PC can switch add-ons off in Settings. Add-ons do not affect who can play with whom.
Other file names are ignored.
